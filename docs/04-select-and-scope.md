**Selected concept and reason:**
* **Selected Concept:** *JoJos Bizarre TCG*
* **Reason:** It has the strongest mechanical hook and player like the jojos anime so it would bring more attention to it. The progression from an un-awakened Crusader/Leader into a full Stand user with mana/shards accumulation creates an exciting mid-game power spike, while face-down trap cards add tactical bluffing.

**Feedback that changed or confirmed my choice:**
Discussions confirmed that players love the transformation fantasy of awakening a Stand once reaching max mana. However, feedback also highlighted that building a full 50-card collectible card game right away is too large of a scope. To make it work, I need to limit the initial prototype to a tight, pre-constructed 20–25 card dual-color deck per player.

**Other ideas to keep for later:**
Original Idea has a meter through out the game that grows and once it hits a certain thresh hold something would happend that would change the game for both player.

**First physical prototype boundary:**
* Two pre-built decks (20–25 cards each) featuring 1 Leader card (Crusader) per deck.
* Fixed 6-card face-down Life pile per player.
* A dedicated pool of 6 Mana/shards cards to test the ramp progression toward Stand Awakening.
* Exactly 2 Stand cards per deck matching the Leader's color identity.
* A focused subset of cards: 4 Units, 4 Traps, 2 Weapons, and 2 Events.

**One feature to defer:**
Defer the full 5-color deck-building system and complex multi-color system for deck building. The initial test will use two fixed, pre-constructed dual-color decks to ensure the core Stand attachment and trap trigger timings works

**One risk to test next:**
Test whether the "Stand Awakening at 6 Mana/shards" rule creates a runaway leader problem, where the first player to find and attach their Stand immediately overpowers the opponent before they can mount a defense.
