# IT265 Module 2: One-Page Treatment

**Working game title:**

Write one coherent, one-page-length treatment. Use the four prompts to begin, then combine your answers into natural prose.

## Four-Sentence Core

1. **Premise:** Players take on the role of rival Crusaders locked in a tactical Stand clash, leading a team of allies and managing their life force to defeat the opposing user.
2. **Experience and loop:** Players repeatedly draw cards, balance mana expenditures between offensive units and hidden face-down traps, and maneuver board power to declare targeted strikes.
3. **Goal and pressure:** The goal is to deplete the opponent’s six-card life pool down to zero before they overwhelm your defenses, under the constant pressure of surprise traps and attacks.
4. **Scope:** This prototype introduces a color-aligned Leader awakening mechanic where reaching maximum mana unlocks your dormant Stand card, testable immediately with two pre-built index card decks.

---

## Treatment Draft

In JoJos Bizarre TCG, two players clash in a duel of tactics and abilities. Each player commands a multi-colored Leader, a Crusader who begins the match unawakened, possessing minor innate abilities and a defensive pool of six face-down cards representing their life force. Through card management, resource pacing, and calculated risks, players goal is to deplete their opponent's life pool to zero to win.

The core gameplay loop centers on energy management, board positioning, and bluffing. At the start of a turn, players draw a card and expand their available mana pool by playing resource shards from hand. With this mana/shards, players decide between establishing presence by deploying unit allies, equipping weapon enhancements to their Leader, or setting tactical cards face-down as traps. Traps remain hidden until specific triggers occur such as intercepting an enemy attack aimed at the player’s life or disrupting an opposing card play forcing opponents to carefully assess hidden threats before committing to combat.

The Stand Awakening mechanic. Decks are capped at two color-aligned Stand cards. Once a player reaches the maximum threshold of 6 mana, if their Stand has not yet been drawn, they may search their deck to awaken and attach their Stand directly to their Leader. This transformation permanently elevates the Leader's power profile and unlocks game-altering abilities—such as extra actions or tempo manipulation. Stand cards drawn beforehand are not wasted dead draws; they can be fielded as active combat units or set into defensive positions until awakening is attained.

Combat resolves by matching and overcoming power values. Attacking units or Leaders must match or exceed the target's defense to inflict damage. When a Leader takes a direct blow, the player draws or discards a card from their six-card life buffer. To prevent unrecoverable board states, if a player’s Stand cards are trapped in their life pool upon reaching 6 mana, a reset trigger reshuffles life and deck cards, conceding an extra draw to the opponent as a risk-reward balance.
