# IT265 Module 2: Concept Pitch Cards

Copy this card for each of your two or three shortlisted concepts. Aim to explain each in about one minute. Use your [idea bank](./01-idea-bank.md) as a starting point.

## Pitch Card

**Working title:**
Jojos Bizzare TCG 

**Player role and situation:**
The player takes turns drawing a card from their deck than play out there turn trying to win the game by making there oppnet hit zero health first.

**Repeated decision or action:**
At the start of each turn, draw a card and gather energy points. Decide how to use the limited energy: play a Stand user character, set face-down trap cards, activate special abilities, or attack opposing cards and the enemy user directly.

**Goal, pressure, or ending:**
* **Goal:** Reduce the opponent's health to zero.
* **Pressure:** Playing card threats on the board and limited energy per turn; choosing between attacking to deal direct damage or defensive trading against opposing cards.
* **Ending:** A player reaches zero health or exhausts their deck with no cards left to draw.

**Hook:**
Hidden bluffing mechanics through face-down trap cards inspired by Stand clashes, forcing players to read their opponent's bluffs before declaring an attack.

**Smallest useful physical prototype:**
A 30-card index card deck with basic stats (Cost, Attack, Health, Effect) and broken shards(tokens) representing Energy and Life totals to test turn flow and combat math on paper.

**Question I want listeners to answer:**
Does having both active character abilities and hidden trap cards add fun mind games, or does it make the first playable prototype too complicated to balance?

**Working title:**
To the Bottom

**Player role and situation:**
Two treasure hunters race down an unstable cave shaft, laying down path and obstacle cards to reach the underground vault while sabotaging each other's has they descened.

**Repeated decision or action:**
Each turn, draw a pathway card and decide whether to play it to extend your personal path deeper toward the treasure, place a cave-in/hazard card to block the opponent's route, or discard to dig through an obstruction.

**Goal, pressure, or ending:**
* **Goal:** Be the first player to form a continuous connected route from the surface down to the vault tile at the bottom.
* **Pressure:** Hand limitations and shared space; spending turns blocking the rival slows them down but sacrifices progress on your own path.
* **Ending:** Reaching the treasure tile completes the game 

**Hook:**
Dual-purpose route building where every card played can either advance your own route or disrupt and rotate your opponent's path tiles.

**Smallest useful physical prototype:**
A grid of square note cards acting as path tiles (straight lines, turns, blockers) and two colored pawns to test path-building speed and block frequency.(this idea can change so the protoyping can change a decent amount)

**Question I want listeners to answer:**
Is the balance between advancing your own path versus blocking the opponent engaging?
