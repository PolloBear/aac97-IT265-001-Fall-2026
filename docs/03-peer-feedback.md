Use one copy for each concept you review. Initials are enough; do not record classmates' contact details. Give feedback on the idea and name a decision the designer can make next.

**Concept:** Jojos Bizarre TCG

**Reviewer initials:** A.D

**In my words, the player repeatedly:**
They havent tested it but told me they like the idea and concpet of the whole tcg.

**One clarifying question:**
When a player reaches 6 mana and searches their deck for a Stand, does that consume their entire turn action, or do they still get to attack?

**Strongest hook or source of appeal:**
The Stand awakening transformation at 6 mana and multi colored aspect.

**What one student could prototype first:**
Two small 20-card index card decks with basic handwritten stats and 6 face-down cards for life to test turn flow.

**Biggest uncertainty or risk to test:**
If one player hits 6 mana first and attaches their Stand, does the match immediately become unwinnable for the other player?

**One actionable suggestion:**
Cap the prototype deck to two colors and pre-construct both decks instead of letting players customize cards so you can test balance quickly.
